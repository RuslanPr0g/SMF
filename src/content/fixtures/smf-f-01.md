## Description

SMF-F-01 is a fixed ladder of eleven rungs. It is structurally sound, unobstructed, and
positioned within reach of the primary approach.

Across 1,204 reviewed sessions, the ladder has been ascended fourteen times. Twelve of those
ascents were reversed within four seconds. The remaining two coincide with sessions in which
the ascending entity was subsequently designated.

The ladder is recorded because its disuse is consistent. Fixtures that specimens ignore
reliably are as informative as fixtures they misuse.
