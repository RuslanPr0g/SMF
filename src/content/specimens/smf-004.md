## Containment Procedures

SMF-004 is not contained by correction. Correction has been attempted and the record of it is
complete: instruction on the matter described below has been issued to the subject by eleven
separate entities across thirty-one sessions, in four cases with the governing figure quoted
directly. No alteration has been recorded. Classification follows from that record and not
from the severity of the behaviour, which is modest.

Recovery objectives in the custody of an active SMF-004 are to be logged as unrecoverable for
the duration of the escort. This is a convenience of record-keeping and is not an instruction
to intervene.

## Description

SMF-004 designates a recurring entity that completes the first half of a recovery reliably and
the second half not at all.

The subject locates and secures a recovery objective without difficulty. Mean elapsed time
from session start to first secure is 41 seconds, the shortest recorded on the floor. Nothing
in this phase of the subject's conduct has ever required comment.

The behaviour of interest begins at the moment the objective is lifted. On securing an
objective, the subject stows the weapon it purchased and draws its blade. Interview establishes
the reasoning without ambiguity: the subject believes the blade increases its rate of travel.

The rate of travel of an entity in custody of a recovery objective is fixed at 200 units per
second. It is not derived from the item held. It is a site constant, imposed at the moment of
lifting and released at the moment of delivery, and it does not vary. An entity carrying an
objective advances at 200 units per second holding a blade. It advances at 200 units per second
holding a rifle. Where a drawn blade would ordinarily permit 250, that figure is suppressed the
instant the objective leaves the floor.

The subject's disarmament is therefore purchased at no gain. The gain is not marginal, and it
is not disputed. It is zero, to the unit.

The second characteristic follows from the first. The subject does not respond to contact.
Across 31 sessions it has been engaged 47 times while in custody of an objective and has drawn
a weapon on none of them. It does not halt, return fire, seek cover, or release the objective.
It continues the route at 200 units per second until stopped by other means. Mean distance
achieved is 1,630 units of a required 3,900, of which the final 400 are covered without
opposition, the opposition having already concluded its work.

Post-session communication is brief and consistent. The phrase "i was going fast" appears in 19
of 31 transcripts.
