## Containment Procedures

SMF-007 in its common form requires no containment. The statement described below is issued
into a channel that has already closed and affects no subsequent round. Observers are advised
against response; none has been recorded to alter the timing, content, or frequency of the
behaviour.

The severe form documented in Addendum 007-B is not a containment matter for this archive. It
was resolved by means outside Site-Office and outside the archive's remit, and is recorded here
because it occurred on the floor, not because the archive claims any part in its resolution.

## Description

SMF-007 designates a behaviour observed at the close of a session, in a subject whose side has
been defeated. The subject issues a statement asserting its own superiority, addressed to no
one in particular, within 2 seconds of the final round resolving. No instance has been recorded
following a session its side won.

The statement itself is nearly invariant in content and is reproduced here in its most common
form: "gg ez." Variation has been logged in only 4 of 210 instances, all cosmetic.

The behaviour is best understood as a response to defeat that reasserts superiority by means
available to the subject at the time. In the overwhelming majority of cases the only means
available is text, and the statement is issued and the matter ends there. Addendum 007-B
documents the one case on file in which the means available were not limited to text.

## Addendum 007-B: Escalated Form, Single Session

A session is on file in which the ordinary statement did not occur, and in its place the
subject produced the only escalation of this behaviour yet recorded.

The subject's side had already been eliminated for the round. One opposing entity remained,
holding Long Hall with a reduced loadout. The subject approached and was eliminated by that
entity in a single exchange, two consecutive headshots, over the full length of the corridor.
Review of the encounter found no irregularity on the surviving entity's part.

No statement was issued. In the round that followed, review of the subject's own session
recording establishes that client-side cheat software was active. The subject recorded a full
elimination of the opposing side in every round from that point until its removal by
administrative action, a span of eleven rounds.

One instance within that span is filed separately from the elimination count. The subject
discharged incendiary utility at the position of an observation post -- an entity of Snowman
Cluster A, beside which SMF-013 was stationed at the time -- with the defeating entity absent
from its field of view at the time of discharge, confirmed by the same recording. Site-Office's standing condition that posts are not to be
obstructed says nothing of a post being fired upon, the case not having previously arisen. The
post was undamaged. It filed as normal.

This is the only recorded instance of a specimen directing hostile action at an observation
post rather than at another specimen. It is being treated here as an escalation of SMF-007
rather than as a new designation, on the grounds that its trigger -- an unaccepted defeat,
reasserted by whatever means were to hand -- is unchanged from the common form.

## Addendum 007-C: Reclassification Proposal

A proposal was submitted following Addendum 007-B to reclassify SMF-007 as requiring active
containment, on the grounds that the escalated form had caused direct interference with an
observation post.

The proposal was denied. The escalated form was resolved by administrative removal before any
containment procedure could have applied, and the common form -- 206 of 210 instances --
remains a text statement with no effect on session state. A single severe instance does not
alter the classification of the two hundred and six that were not.

Classification stands at **Uninstall**.
