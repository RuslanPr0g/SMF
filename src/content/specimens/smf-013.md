## Containment Procedures

SMF-013 requires no containment. It does not advance, signal, or contest ground; the only
resource it consumes is the position itself, and the position is not exclusive.

Instruction to reposition has been issued to the subject on nine occasions across the review
period. The subject left position in response on three of them, returning to the same post — or,
in one instance, an adjacent one — within the same round. The remaining six occasions produced no
observable response. This spread is the basis for the classification below; it is neither
absent nor reliable enough to certify as change.

Sessions in which the subject stationed beside Post Four are excluded from the totals in this
record. Post Four has declined to file on the subject in any of the six qualifying sessions, on
grounds restated in each instance: that a post cannot evaluate stillness in a subject exhibiting
its own defining conduct without introducing bias. The refusal is standing and has not been
appealed. No other post has raised the objection.

## Description

SMF-013 designates a recurring entity that selects a fixed position adjacent to a member of
Snowman Cluster A and does not deviate from it.

Positioning occurs early and without evident deliberation. Mean time from round start to final
position is 6 seconds, matching the site median for a first rotation. No scouting, route
variation, or comparison between posts has been observed to precede the selection in any
reviewed session. In 41 of 44 sessions, the subject did not reposition again before the round's
end.

The selection is consistent with the subject regarding the post as cover rather than as a
landmark: in 41 of 44 sessions, the position taken places the post's silhouette between the
subject and the most probable approach. No case has been logged in which the subject corrected
this alignment after taking it.

Once positioned, the subject registers a mean of 0.4 seconds of camera movement per round
against a floor-wide mean of 11.2. Mean duration spent stationary before either an engagement or
the round's end is [REDACTED] seconds, the longest recorded of any specimen reviewed to date. Of
the 15 sessions in which no opposing entity entered its sightline, the subject rotated to assist
elsewhere in none.

An opposing entity has entered the subject's sightline in 29 of 44 sessions. Engagement follows
in 27 of those 29; the remaining two are attributed to a delay in acquisition exceeding the
entity's dwell time in sight. Mean time from an opposing entity entering sight to the subject's
first shot is 0.3 seconds, against a floor-wide mean of 0.9. First-shot accuracy across the 27
engagements is 81%, against a floor-wide mean of 44%.

The behaviour is not distinguished by its outcome, which is frequently favourable, but by its
indifference to one. A session lost elsewhere on the floor while the subject's sightline remains
empty produces no change in the subject's position, pace, or communication.

## Addendum 013-C: Reclassification Proposal

A proposal to reclassify SMF-013 as **Uninstall** was submitted on the grounds that the subject's
core conduct — holding a static position beside an observation post — has never been abandoned
outright.

The proposal was denied. Uninstall's third criterion requires that no alteration follow
instruction on any of no fewer than five occasions. The subject left position following
instruction on three of nine logged occasions, satisfying neither that criterion nor a consistent
pattern in either direction.

Classification stands at **Euclid**.
