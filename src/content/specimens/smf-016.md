## Containment Procedures

SMF-016 is filed as one record with two sub-designations. This arrangement has been challenged
four times and upheld four times; the reasoning is set out in Addendum 016-D and is not
revisited here.

No containment procedure is in force. Four proposals to establish one have been returned,
in each case on the grounds that the applicant had not identified what the procedure would be
containing.

Observers are reminded that classification records whether a behaviour changes under
instruction and records nothing else. It does not record severity. It does not record
frequency. It does not record the observer's assessment of the subject. This reminder has been
appended to the present record at the direction of post four, following review of the language
in three withdrawn proposals.

## Description

SMF-016 designates a paired entity, its components filed as α and β. Neither component has been
observed to exhibit the behaviour in the absence of the other, and separation trials are
recorded in Addendum 016-D.

The pairing is dormant for the majority of sessions in which both components are present. It
does not activate on round number, on side, on score, or on the composition of the opposing
force. It activates when the combined round contribution of the three other co-located entities
falls below 0.4 and remains below it for two consecutive rounds. Across the review period this
condition has been met 38 times, and the pairing has activated on 38 of them.

On activation both components procure, within the same second, the following and nothing else:

- twin sidearms, priced at 300
- body armour without helmet, priced at 650
- on some activations, one decoy grenade, priced at 50

The upper bound of the pairing's procurement is therefore 1,000 per component. Reserve at
session end has exceeded 12,000 in 22 of the 38 activations. No procurement above 1,000 has
ever been logged for either component, at any reserve.

The weapon selected requires six torso hits to remove an armoured entity and is the least
expensive item in the interface that is not utility. The armour selected leaves the head
unprotected, and both components are removed by single head contact from any rifle on the
floor. Neither component has been observed to procure a helmet, and the 350 required for one
has been present in reserve in every logged activation.

Following activation, the pairing resolves the session by elimination. The recovery objective
is not approached. Across 38 activations the objective has been recovered zero times, by either
component, at any point.

Sessions in which SMF-016 activates are resolved in its side's favour in 31 of 38 instances.
Sessions in comparable deficit in which the pairing does not activate are resolved in the
side's favour in [REDACTED]% of instances. The comparative figure is withheld pending review of
the method by which it was obtained. Three reviews have been completed. The figure remains
withheld.

## Addendum 016-B: The Decoy

The decoy grenade is procured on 14 of 38 activations. The condition governing this has not
been established.

Nineteen variables have been tested against the fourteen instances: side, round number, score
differential, reserve, opposing procurement, session duration to activation, objective
position, time of session, composition of the opposing force, and ten further measures listed
in the working file. None correlates. The distribution is not periodic. It is not alternating.
It does not follow the preceding session's outcome.

The decoy, when procured, is thrown in 3 of 14 instances and retained in 11. The three throws
occurred at positions of no observed tactical relevance. In two of them the pairing was not
under contact and did not come under contact for a further 40 seconds.

No further variables are proposed for testing. The working file remains open.

exists only in the pair.
