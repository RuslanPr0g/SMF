## Containment Procedures

SMF-015 is inert for the majority of any given session and requires no procedure while in that
state. The subject does not move, does not communicate, and does not respond to communication
directed at it. Eleven attempts to raise the subject during an inactive period are on record.
None received a reply.

The inert state ends the instant the subject's side reserve reaches 5,050 -- the combined price
of one decoy grenade and one G3SG1. Observers are advised that this figure, not round number,
not team composition, not the state of the session in progress, is the entire trigger condition.
It has held across all 74 logged activations.

No procedure exists for the active state. The subject does not deviate from its route under any
observed condition, including direct address, and eleven further attempts made during activation
also went unanswered.

## Description

For the majority of a session in which its side's reserve is below 5,050, SMF-015 does not act.
It holds its spawn position, issues no purchase, and does not respond on any channel. This
period has been described by co-located entities as indistinguishable from disconnection, and
the subject's connection has been verified as active throughout.

It is also indistinguishable, over this period, from SMF-012, which is inert on the same terms
and does not subsequently activate. Attributions made before the threshold are provisional in
both directions.

On the round in which reserve first reaches 5,050, the subject procures a decoy grenade and a
G3SG1 without exception, in that order, within the first three seconds of the buy window. No
armour has been procured in any of the 74 logged activations; the reserve required for both
items does not reliably leave enough for it, and the subject has not been observed to wait a
further round to afford one.

The subject then proceeds directly to Conference Room, on foot, with the walk key held for the
full duration of the route. It does not vary the route between activations. It does not respond
to contact, does not slow at junctions, and does not communicate at any point before, during, or
after. The decoy is not thrown.

On arrival the subject holds one position with total consistency: coordinates 112, 46.4, 54.
The fractional value on the vertical axis is not an error in observation. It is the resting
elevation of a crouched stance reached by holding the walk key continuously into a stop, and it
recurs to the same figure in all 74 instances, including the ones in which the subject did not
survive to occupy it.

## Addendum 015-C: Reclassification Proposal

A proposal to reclassify SMF-015 as **Coachable** was submitted on the grounds that a subject
inactive for the majority of a session cannot be said to be resisting instruction, since no
instruction is being received.

The proposal was denied. The class requires that instruction be issued and observed to have no
effect, not that the subject be reachable. Instruction was issued on eleven occasions during
inactivity and eleven more during activation. Twenty-two occasions, zero effect, satisfies the
criterion regardless of the subject's apparent absence.

Classification stands at **Uninstall**.
