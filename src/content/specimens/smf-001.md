## Containment Procedures

SMF-001 requires no containment, and no containment is possible. Containment of a procurement
behaviour depends on the behaviour being expensive, and this one is not. The weapon the subject
favours is priced at 1,700 — below every rifle available to either side — and lies within the
subject's reserve in every round after the first. No economic mechanism has been observed to
interrupt the behaviour. None is expected to.

Guidance issued to the subject by co-located entities has been logged on sixty-one occasions.
In fifty-nine, the subject repeated its procurement in the following round. In the remaining
two, the subject procured a second belt-fed weapon and transferred the first to a teammate.

Observers are reminded that procurement guidance falls outside the observational remit. The
sixty-one instances above were issued by entities on the floor and are recorded, not endorsed.

## Description

SMF-001 designates a recurring entity that proceeds to the procurement interface ahead of any
other action. Mean time from round start to procurement is 4.2 seconds, against a floor-wide
mean of 11.8.

The subject procures a belt-fed weapon in the first round its reserve permits one. Across 40
reviewed sessions this was the second round in 31 instances and the third in the remaining 9.
It re-procures the same weapon in every subsequent round, including rounds in which the
previous instance was recovered intact.

The distinguishing characteristic is not the weapon. It is what accompanies the weapon, which
is nothing. A complete utility loadout — one flashbang, one smoke, one high-explosive — is
priced at 800, less than half the weapon the subject has already committed to. Armour is
priced at 1,000. The subject's reserve at the moment of procurement has been sufficient for
both in 94% of reviewed rounds. Neither has been procured.

Across 392 logged rounds, the subject has thrown zero grenades. On a site where the recovery
objective is contested through two stairwells and a single glazed corridor, this is not a
matter of preference.

The behaviour that gives the subject its designation is sustained fire in the absence of an
identified target. Audio records place mean trigger duration at 3.1 seconds, against a floor
mean of 0.4. Accuracy across 340 logged engagements is 6.8%. The subject's casualty
contribution to its own side is 1.4 per session, a figure exceeding its contribution to the
opposing side in 38% of sessions reviewed.

Subject displays no awareness of the discrepancy. Post-session communication is limited and
consistently attributes the outcome to teammates who did not follow.

A secondary characteristic is positional. SMF-001 does not hold a position; it advances, and
where advance is not possible it fires into the surface obstructing advance. Damage to
Site-Office fixtures is negligible. Damage to the acoustic environment is not.

## Addendum 001-C: Reclassification Proposal

A proposal to reclassify SMF-001 as **Coachable** was submitted following a single session in
which the subject procured a rifle, armour, and a complete utility loadout.

The proposal was denied. Review established that the subject threw no grenade at any point in
that session and held all three at its conclusion, and that it resumed its standard
procurement in the session following. The rifle has not been procured again.

Classification stands at **Uninstall**.
