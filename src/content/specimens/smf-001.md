## Containment Procedures

SMF-001 requires no containment. Observation has established that the subject contains
itself: procurement of a belt-fed weapon in the opening round removes 5,700 units of
purchasing capacity from the subject's side for a minimum of two subsequent rounds, and the
resulting deficit is self-correcting only in the sense that it ends the session sooner.

Observers are instructed not to issue purchasing guidance over voice. Sixty-one instances of
such guidance have been logged. In fifty-nine, the subject repeated the procurement in the
following round. In the remaining two, the subject procured a second belt-fed weapon and
transferred the first to a teammate.

Personnel who identify SMF-001 in the opening thirty seconds of a session are to record the
designation and continue as normal.

## Description

SMF-001 designates a recurring entity that appears on Site-Office at session start and
proceeds directly to the procurement interface. The subject exhibits no hesitation at this
stage; mean time from session start to procurement is 4.2 seconds, against a floor-wide mean
of 11.8.

The distinguishing behaviour is sustained fire in the absence of an identified target. Audio
records place mean trigger duration at 3.1 seconds, against a floor mean of 0.4. Accuracy
across 340 logged engagements is 6.8%. The subject's own casualty contribution to its side is
1.4 per session, a figure that exceeds its contribution to the opposing side in 38% of
sessions reviewed.

Subject displays no awareness of the discrepancy. Post-session communication is limited and
consistently attributes the outcome to teammates who did not follow.

A secondary characteristic is positional. SMF-001 does not hold a position; it advances, and
where advance is not possible it fires into the surface obstructing advance. Damage to
Site-Office fixtures is negligible. Damage to the acoustic environment is not.

## Addendum 001-C: Reclassification Proposal

A proposal to reclassify SMF-001 as **Coachable** was submitted following a session in which
the subject procured a rifle in round 1 and finished with a positive contribution.

The proposal was denied. Review established that the subject had procured the rifle in error,
having selected an adjacent entry in the procurement interface, and that it corrected the
error in round 2 at a cost of [REDACTED] units.
