## Containment Procedures

No procedure is in force and none has been drafted. Four proposals were begun and abandoned at
the same point, that being the section requiring the applicant to state the behaviour to be
contained.

The record's status is addressed in Addendum 012-D and is not a matter of incompleteness.

## Description

SMF-012 designates a recurring entity that does not act.

It procures nothing. It issues nothing on any channel. Its position at session end is identical
to its position at session start in 71 of 74 reviewed sessions; in the remaining three the
displacement is attributable to the movement of a co-located entity through the same space and
not to the subject. Its view direction across the full review period has changed on no occasion
that has been recorded.

The subject is not disconnected. Connection has been verified as active in all 74 sessions, and
in 68 of them the client reported input events consistent with a present operator. What those
inputs were directed at has not been established.

## Addendum 012-A: Distinction from SMF-015

SMF-012 is repeatedly confused with SMF-015, and the confusion is not an error of observation.
For the opening rounds of any session the two are identical in every measurable respect:
neither procures, neither moves, neither communicates, neither responds. There is no measurement
that separates them and none has been proposed.

The separation occurs at one point only. On reaching a side reserve of 5,050, SMF-015 activates.
SMF-012 does not. Identification is therefore retrospective, and available only in sessions
where the threshold is reached at all.

Across the review period, 23 sessions were logged as SMF-015 and subsequently reattributed to
SMF-012. A further 9 ended before reserve reached 5,050 and remain unattributed. They will not
be attributed.

The confusion arises only in T-side sessions, SMF-015 being exclusively T-side. That it arises
at all in the other half suggests observers are not consulting the field.

## Addendum 012-B: Findings of the Cluster A Working Group

A working group was convened at the direction of post four to establish whether SMF-012 and
SMF-015 are one specimen or two. Its findings are reproduced.

> Let a subject's conduct over an interval be null where no procurement, movement, transmission
> or response occurs within it.
>
> For SMF-015, conduct is null on every interval preceding the reserve threshold and non-null on
> every interval containing it. For SMF-012, conduct is null on every interval.
>
> The two are therefore identical on all intervals not containing the threshold, and distinct on
> all intervals containing it.
>
> **Corollary.** In a session where the threshold is not reached, no distinction between the two
> exists in the evidence. The subjects are not difficult to separate in such a session. They are
> not separable.
>
> **On distinctness across the corpus.** SMF-012's side reserve exceeded 5,050 in 61 of the 74
> reviewed sessions. No activation followed in any. Taking activation at the threshold as
> equiprobable, the likelihood of 61 consecutive non-activations under a single-specimen
> hypothesis is 1 in 2,305,843,009,213,693,952.
>
> The specimens are distinct. This is the finding of the group.

The assumption that activation at the threshold is equiprobable has been questioned by two
posts. The working group has declined to revise it, and has been dissolved.

## Addendum 012-C: The Four Eliminations

Four eliminations are recorded to SMF-012 across the review period. No mechanism has been
established for any of them.

The subject's view direction did not change during any of the four. In one, the client reported
no input event for 41 seconds either side of the elimination. In another, the eliminated entity
was not within the subject's field of view at any point in the round.

The eliminations are recorded because they occurred. They are not explained because they have
not been.

## Addendum 012-D: Status of the Record

This record is held as preliminary and will remain so.

The status does not reflect insufficient observation. SMF-012 is the most extensively reviewed
specimen on file, at 74 sessions against a mean of 39, and the volume of material is not the
obstacle.

The obstacle is the corollary in Addendum 012-B. A full profile requires that the subject be
identified as SMF-012 rather than as SMF-015 at the time of observation, and the corollary
establishes that no observation conducted before the threshold can do this. Every session
attributed to SMF-012 was attributed after the fact, by the absence of an event.

A profile approved on that basis would be a profile of what a subject did not do, assembled from
sessions in which it was not known which subject was being watched.

Post four has declined to approve one. The record stands as it is.
