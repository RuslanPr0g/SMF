## Containment Procedures

SMF-014 is not contained by cost. Its loadout — a Nova and a single decoy, 1,100 combined — is
within reserve from the second round onward and has never once been withheld by the subject's
own economy. No mechanism has been observed to interrupt procurement on this basis.

Correction has been attempted independently of cost. Instruction concerning ammunition
conservation has been issued to the subject by six separate entities across nine occasions
spanning the 44 sessions reviewed below. No alteration has been recorded following any
instance, and none is expected: the subject's non-combat conduct, described below, indicates a
low degree of engagement with a round's outcome irrespective of the instruction it receives.

The designation above is not the subject's. It is drawn from the volume of image captures
logged to the client following elimination, a body of material observers have taken to calling
paper for lack of a more precise term. No formal review of the material has been undertaken; it
falls outside the containment remit.

## Description

SMF-014 designates a recurring entity that concludes its participation in a round before that
round's contest has properly begun.

At round start the subject procures a Nova and a single decoy grenade and advances directly
into mid. Armour is purchased in none of the 44 reviewed sessions. Mean time from round start to
first shot fired is 3.1 seconds, against a floor-wide mean of 9.9. The lane the subject occupies
at the moment of firing has, in every reviewed instance, been unoccupied.

The subject discharges into this lane without a confirmed target. Mean shells expended before an
opposing entity is confirmed present is 27 of a possible 40 — the Nova's full loadout, chamber
and reserve combined — and in 38 of 44 rounds the subject exhausts its ammunition entirely
before any target is acquired. Confirmed hits from this volume of fire: zero.

Contact, when it occurs, is therefore met by a subject that is unarmoured and, in the majority
of reviewed rounds, unarmed. Time to elimination following first contact has a mean of 0.6
seconds, against a floor-wide mean of 2.9. The subject has not survived first contact in any of
the 44 sessions reviewed.

The behaviour that gives the subject its designation begins at the point of elimination, not
before it. Freed of engagement, the subject enters spectator mode and remains there for the
balance of the round, mean duration 71 seconds. During this interval its screenshot function
registers a mean of 6.4 activations per round, against a floor-wide mean of 0.2. The subject's
cumulative screenshot count across the review period stands at [REDACTED], a figure exceeding
the combined total logged for every other reviewed specimen. Zero instances of positional or
tactical information relayed by the subject during spectation have been logged across the 44
sessions reviewed, though voice and text channels remain open to it throughout.

Post-session sentiment is uniform and does not track the session's result. Across 44
transcripts, the subject's characterisation of a session is favourable in 41 and neutral in the
remaining three; it has not been recorded as unfavourable regardless of round or match outcome.

## Addendum 014-C: Reclassification Proposal

A proposal to reclassify SMF-014 as **Coachable** was submitted following a session in which
the subject, informed in writing of its ammunition-exhaustion rate, held its final decoy in
reserve for the full round and discharged no shells into mid.

The proposal was denied. Review established that the subject had entered spectator mode at
00:04, having been eliminated by an opposing entity it neither fired on nor was aware had
entered the lane; the altered behaviour was not instruction taking effect but the subject's
standard post-elimination conduct beginning earlier than usual. In the session immediately
following, the subject's full pattern resumed.

Classification stands at **Uninstall**.
