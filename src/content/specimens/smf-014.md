## Containment Procedures

SMF-014 requires no containment. The subject confines itself, to a degree no procedure of this
archive could improve upon, and has never been observed outside a room measuring 640 units on
its longest axis.

Instruction to reposition has been issued to the subject by seven separate entities across
fourteen occasions in the 44 sessions reviewed. Four of these specified the recovery point by
name. No alteration has been recorded.

The designation is observational. It refers to the room.

## Description

SMF-014 designates a recurring entity that reaches Paper within 12 seconds of round start,
occupies it, and does not leave it.

Displacement is minimal and is the most consistent measure on file: across 44 sessions the
subject's maximum recorded distance from a single fixed point within Paper is 340 units. It
does not rotate to adjacent positions. It does not withdraw under pressure. It does not advance
when the room is clear.

The subject procures a Nova and a single decoy, 1,100 combined, in every reviewed session.
Armour is procured in none. The weapon is a close-range instrument, and Paper is a close-range
room: stacked stock, obstructed lines, and a single entrance. The pairing of the two is, on
paper, correct.

The subject does not use it that way. From its fixed position the subject discharges toward mid,
through the doorway and across Cross, at a mean engagement range of 1,410 units. At that range
the weapon's pellets do not carry. Mean damage registered per shell that connects at all is 4.
Across the review period the subject has expended 1,180 shells in this direction and achieved 0
eliminations by it.

The room the subject is standing in is the range at which the weapon functions. Opposing
entities entered Paper on 19 occasions across the review period. The subject was oriented toward
mid on 19 of them. It turned on 3, in each case after contact had been made, and secured 1
elimination in total — the only elimination on its record, achieved at 210 units by a subject
that had not chosen to engage at that distance.

Conduct does not vary with side. Assigned to hold, the subject's position is consistent with the
assignment and its orientation is not. Assigned to recover, the subject's position is not
consistent with the assignment in any respect, the recovery point lying outside the room. The
subject's conduct is therefore correct by accident in approximately half of sessions, and was so
in 21 of the 44 reviewed.

The decoy is retained in 44 of 44 sessions and is recovered unthrown at session end.

## Addendum 014-C: Reclassification Proposal

A proposal to reclassify SMF-014 as **Coachable** was submitted following a single session in
which the subject was recorded outside Paper.

The proposal was denied. Review established that the session in question had placed the
subject's spawn outside the room, and that the recording in question was of the subject
travelling toward Paper at [REDACTED] seconds, which it then occupied for the remainder of the
session. Arriving at Paper is not leaving Paper.

Classification stands at **Uninstall**.
