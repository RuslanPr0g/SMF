## Containment Procedures

SMF-017 requires no containment and responds to none. The subject is unusual among filed
specimens in that it does absorb instruction: guidance offered to it is acknowledged, restated
in its own terms, and incorporated. It is incorporated into the model. The model then produces
a further commitment, and the further commitment is also incorrect.

Observers are directed to the class criteria before proposing a reclassification on this
basis. Revision is not correction. A specimen that updates is not thereby improving.

The designation above is the subject's own. It appears in communication logs on 41 occasions
prior to any observer recording it. No qualification of any kind has been established, and the
subject has not been asked to produce one.

## Description

SMF-017 designates a recurring entity that will not enter an engagement it has not first
resolved.

Prior to commitment the subject conducts an evaluation. Interview establishes that the subject
regards this evaluation as exhaustive: it reports having reviewed 14,000,605 outcomes before a
single engagement, a figure it has restated without variation on nine separate occasions. Mean
deliberation is 31 seconds, against a floor-wide mean of 1.4.

The evaluation terminates in a single selected outcome. The subject then commits to that
outcome in full: it discharges into the position it has selected, at the interval it has
calculated, before the opposing entity has disclosed itself. The commitment is total. The
subject has never been observed to hold a second possibility in reserve, and describes doing so
as inefficient.

Across 612 logged commitments the selected position has been correct on 0 occasions.

The subject's own position, by contrast, is nearly invariant. In 587 of 612 sessions -- 96% --
SMF-017 is found in Long Hall, weapon raised on the doorway giving onto the hostage corridor,
and reaches this position within the first 15 seconds of the round. It does not relocate once
set. The evaluation the subject reports running each round appears to model every variable of
the engagement to come except the one under its own control.

The subject's evaluation is not uniformly poor. Weapon carried is correctly identified in 71%
of cases and approach side in 63%. Position is identified in none. The subject's model requires
the location of an entity that has not yet disclosed its location, and no quantity of
deliberation resolves this. Deliberation extends the interval during which the location may
change.

The subject's stated confidence at the moment of commitment has never been recorded below
[REDACTED]%. It has never been recorded to fall following an incorrect commitment. Post-session
communication takes the form of a revision notice: the subject reports that the model has been
updated and that the following session will benefit.

612 revisions have been logged. The benefit has not been observed.

demonstrably incorporates instruction, satisfying the first criterion of that class.

reasoning has, 612 times.
