## Facility

Site-Office is a two-level commercial structure held under continuous observation since
commissioning. Its function is not the subject of this archive. The entities that appear
within it are.

The structure is unusual among monitored sites in that it is not sealed. Entities enter at
fixed points at fixed intervals and are not prevented from doing so. Observation has never
established what draws them. The prevailing view is that nothing does, and that the site is
simply available.

## Observation Posts

Observation is conducted by Snowman Cluster A, stationed beyond the northern and eastern
glazing. Four posts are active.

| Post  | Position                             | Status          |
| ----- | ------------------------------------ | --------------- |
| One   | Northern glazing, ground level       | Active          |
| Two   | Northern glazing, ground level, west | Active          |
| Three | Eastern glazing, ground level        | Active          |
| Four  | Second level, northern aspect        | Active. Senior. |

Post four has held station since commissioning and has not been observed to change position.
Seniority within the cluster is determined by continuity of station. Post four's authority is
therefore absolute and, to date, unexercised.

## Standing Conditions

1. Posts observe. Posts do not intervene, signal, or obstruct.
2. Records are filed by the post that made the observation. Second-hand filing is returned.
3. Withdrawal of a post from station requires clearance from post four. No application has
   been submitted.
