## Summary

At 00:14 into session 4412, SMF-004 initiated recovery of the eastern hostage. Recovery was
completed at 00:31. The subject stowed its rifle, drew its blade, and commenced escort without
survey. This is consistent with its profile and required no further note at the time.

At 00:38 SMF-001 entered the northern service area behind the escort and commenced sustained
fire along the corridor. The corridor was unoccupied. Observation confirms no opposing entity
was present in the northern service area at any point before 01:02.

At 00:41 the escort altered course toward the fire. Post-session interview establishes that
SMF-004 did not identify the fire as contact and did not alter its intent; the route it was
following passes through the northern service area, and it was following the route. The blade
remained drawn. Both the hostage and SMF-004 were lost at 00:47, at a position 2.1 metres from
SMF-F-01.
